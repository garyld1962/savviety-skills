**From:** Sarah Chen, VP of Engineering Operations
**To:** Business Analysis Team
**Subject:** Technical Debt Tracking — Need a Solution

Team,

We need a way to get technical debt under control across the organization. Right now every BU and team tracks it differently — some use spreadsheets, some use Jira labels, some don't track it at all. Leadership keeps asking how much tech debt we have and what it's costing us, and we can't answer that confidently.

What we need is an application that gives us:

- A **central inventory** of technical debt items across all business units and teams
- The ability to **filter by BU and team** so both leaders and individual teams can see their own view
- A way to **prioritize** debt items based on risk, cost, and business impact
- **Risk assessment** for each item — what happens if we don't address it
- **Cost estimation** — rough effort/cost to remediate each item
- **Mitigation planning** — what are we doing about it, who's assigned, what's the timeline
- **Scheduling and assignment** — ability to assign debt items to teams and schedule them into sprints or quarters

The VP of Engineering and the CTO have both flagged this as a priority for Q3. We've been burned twice this year by outages that traced back to known tech debt nobody was tracking centrally. The last incident cost us ~$200K in lost revenue and took 3 teams offline for a week.

Several teams have asked if we can just buy something off the shelf. Others want to build it internally since "we know our process best." I don't have a strong opinion either way — I just need it to work and I need adoption across 8 BUs and ~40 engineering teams.

Happy to discuss further. Let me know what you need from me.

— Sarah
